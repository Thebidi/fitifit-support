Delete Your Fitifit Account

You can delete your Fitifit account directly inside the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Fitifit app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

What data is deleted

When you delete your account, your profile, workout history, meal logs, food scan history, trainer groups, assigned plans, progress data, and other Fitifit account data are permanently deleted.

What data may be retained

Some purchase, billing, or transaction records may be retained by Apple, Google Play, RevenueCat, or payment processors as required by law or platform policies.
